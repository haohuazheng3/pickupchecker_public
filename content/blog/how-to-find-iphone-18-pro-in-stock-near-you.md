Online delivery estimates for a new iPhone stretch to weeks within hours of launch. Stores are different: most receive stock several mornings a week, and apple.com lists it for **in-store pickup** the moment it is scanned in.

## Where pickup stock shows up

On any iPhone buy page, choose your configuration, then **Pick up** and enter a ZIP code. Apple shows the 12 nearest stores and whether each can hand you the phone today. That list is the ground truth; it is what PickupChecker reads for all 269 US stores at once.

## When it appears

- Early morning, when overnight deliveries are processed.
- Mid-day, when unclaimed reservations are released.
- Randomly, when an order is cancelled.

## How to be first

- Decide your acceptable configurations in advance: two colors and two storage sizes multiply your chances.
- Keep your Apple ID signed in on your phone and a card ready in Apple Pay.
- Use an alert instead of refreshing. [Set one up](/#notify): one email, seconds after the flip, with the store and a reserve link.
